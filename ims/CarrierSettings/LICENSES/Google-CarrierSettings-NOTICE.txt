Google CarrierSettings data

The XML files under assets/carrier_settings are a filtered transformation of
Google CarrierSettings protobuf data. SOURCE.md describes the data's origin and
schema; carrier_settings_source.json records input and output hashes.

The imported source data was not accompanied by a separate data license. This
notice records provenance and does not grant a new license or relicense the
data under the Apache-2.0 license of the conversion tools and protobuf schemas.
Soong records these data assets using its legacy_proprietary license kind.
